Where sales quietly leak — and how MerchLight helps

Most problems don't break your store. They just make it sell less. MerchLight checks your store and Google automatically, on a schedule, and turns what it finds into a clear plan.

Your Shopify store

Products, inventory and orders — every one of them.

  • Best-sellers that ran out of stock, with the sales at stake
  • Live products with no inventory
  • Variants without a SKU and products without images
  • Paid orders waiting too long to ship, and shipped orders without tracking
  • Your best-seller and your total sales for the month
  • Your whole catalog and recent orders, with no size limit

Google Merchant Center

Why your products don't show on Google.

  • Disapproved products and the reason Google gives
  • Account settings that block products, like shipping or country setup
  • Missing details that limit visibility, like age group, gender and color
  • The full list of affected products, ready to export
  • A separate Google score, so you know how healthy your listings are

The MerchLight advantage

Everything in one place, ranked by what it costs you.

  • One health score from 0 to 100 that lights up as your store improves
  • Sales at risk shown in money, based on your real sales
  • Clear steps for every issue — you stay in control of every change
  • Automatic scans that confirm what you resolved and catch anything new
  • Ignore with a safety net: resolved issues come back if they're still there after 7 days
  • Read-only and private: no customer names, emails or addresses

How it works

Set up in a few minutes. No code, no changes to your theme.

  1. Install and connect

    Add MerchLight to your Shopify store and, if you sell on Google, connect Merchant Center.

  2. Get your health score

    One scan gives you a score for your store and for Google, with every problem ranked by what it costs you.

  3. Resolve and watch it improve

    Each problem tells you what to change and how. Automatic scans confirm what you resolved and catch anything new.

Built to read, never to change

  • Read-only access to your store. MerchLight never edits products, orders or settings.
  • No customer names, emails, phone numbers or addresses.
  • Google connections are encrypted and can be disconnected at any time.
  • Uninstall and your data is deleted within 48 hours. Read our privacy policy.
