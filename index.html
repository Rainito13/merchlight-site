What MerchLight does

MerchLight is a Shopify app that checks your store and your Google Merchant Center automatically for problems that cost you sales — products out of stock, orders waiting to ship, missing SKUs and images, and products Google has disapproved. It gives your store a health score from 0 to 100, ranks every problem by its impact, and explains exactly what to change. MerchLight only reads your data: you make every change yourself, in Shopify or Google.

Where sales quietly leak — and how MerchLight helps

Most problems don't break your store. They just make it sell less. MerchLight checks your store and Google automatically, on a schedule, and turns what it finds into a clear plan.

Your Shopify store

Products, inventory and orders — every one of them.

  • Best-sellers that ran out of stock, with the sales at stake
  • Live products with no inventory
  • Variants without a SKU and products without images
  • Paid orders waiting too long to ship, and shipped orders without tracking
  • Your best-seller and your total sales for the month
  • Your whole catalog and recent orders, with no size limit

Google Merchant Center

Why your products don't show on Google.

  • Disapproved products and the reason Google gives
  • Account settings that block products, like shipping or country setup
  • Missing details that limit visibility, like age group, gender and color
  • The full list of affected products, ready to export
  • A separate Google score, so you know how healthy your listings are

How it works

Set up in a few minutes. No code, no changes to your theme.

  1. Install and run your first scan

    Add MerchLight to your Shopify store. Your first scan gives you a health score from 0 to 100 — and your first trophy, Bronze.

  2. Connect Google, if you sell there

    Optionally connect Google Merchant Center to see which products Google has disapproved or limited, and why.

  3. Follow your priorities

    Start with your next best move. Every issue shows its points, its impact and how long it takes, with clear steps to resolve it.

  4. Watch your store light up

    Automatic scans confirm what you resolved and catch anything new. Your score rises, the bulbs light up and you earn new trophies.

How MerchLight uses Google data

Only if you choose to connect Google Merchant Center.

  • MerchLight reads the status of your products and account in Merchant Center: which products are disapproved or limited, where, and why.
  • It uses this only to show you those problems inside the app, with guidance, a Google score and an optional CSV export.
  • It is read-only: it never creates, changes or deletes anything in your Merchant Center account.

Your data stays yours

We follow the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • Google data is never sold, never used for advertising and never shared with third parties.
  • You can disconnect Google at any time from the app.
  • Read the full details in our privacy policy.

Built to read, never to change

  • Read-only access to your store. MerchLight never edits products, orders or settings.
  • No customer names, emails, phone numbers or addresses.
  • Google connections are encrypted and can be disconnected at any time.
