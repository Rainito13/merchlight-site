What you're looking at

Three things tell you, at a glance, how your store is doing and where to start.

  1. A health score that lights up

    One score for your whole store, from 0 to 100. The lamp fills as it rises, and your light level shows how close you are to glowing. Keep your store healthy over time and you earn trophies: Bronze, Silver, Gold and Diamond.

  2. Your next best move

    Not sure where to start? MerchLight picks the issue that raises your score the most for the least effort — high-impact issues first — and tells you how long it takes.

  3. Points for every issue

    Each priority shows how many points it's worth. Resolve them all and you reach 100. Every resolved issue lights up a bulb, and automatic scans confirm what you fixed.

Clear guidance for every issue

Open any issue to see why it matters, how to resolve it, and exactly which products are affected. MerchLight never edits your store — it shows you what to change, and the next scan confirms it's done.

  • Why it matters — what the problem costs you.
  • How to resolve it — short, practical steps.
  • Affected products — each one with a direct link.
  • Ignore, with a safety net — mark an issue as already resolved and it stops counting in your score. If it's still there after 7 days, it comes back.

Working for you in the background

Every product, every order

A quick scan answers in seconds. A full scan then checks your entire catalog and recent orders in the background — from a few products to hundreds of thousands — and the dashboard updates by itself.

Automatic scans

MerchLight checks your store on a schedule, so new problems are caught early and you can see what you've already resolved.

Built to read, never to change

  • Read-only access. MerchLight never edits products, orders or settings.
  • No customer names, emails, phone numbers or addresses.
  • Google connections are encrypted and can be disconnected at any time.
  • Read our privacy policy.
