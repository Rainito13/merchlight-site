01

What MerchLight does

MerchLight finds the problems that quietly cost your store sales, puts a number on them, and tells you which one to fix first.

The goal

Optimise your store as far as it can go, so your conversion rate climbs.

Every visitor you've paid for, through ads, Google or social, should land on a product they can buy, find what they need to decide, and get their order on time. Each MerchLight check removes one reason a shopper leaves without buying.

It checks

Your products, stock, images, SKUs and orders in Shopify, how fast your public store loads and its trust pages, plus Google Merchant Center, Google Ads and Meta Ads if you connect them.

It scores

Everything it finds becomes one health score out of 100, shown as a light bulb: the brighter, the healthier.

02

Getting started

Five minutes from install to your first score.

  1. Install MerchLightOpen it from Apps in your Shopify admin. Nothing is scanned until you ask.
  2. Press “Run scan”A quick scan of your first 100 products and 100 most recent orders appears in seconds. A full scan of every product and the last 60 days of orders then runs in the background, and the page updates by itself when it's done.
  3. Answer a few quick questionsThe lamp asks whether you sell in physical stores and how you fulfil orders (yourself or a dropshipping supplier). Once ads are connected, it also asks which period should count for your ads (7, 15 or 30 days) and your average margin. If shoppers leave checkouts and MerchLight can't tell who sends the reminder emails, it asks that too: Shopify, an email app like Omnisend or Klaviyo, or nobody yet. Your answers tailor the advice.
  4. Follow “Get set up”A checklist on the Overview: connect Google Merchant Center, Google Ads and Meta Ads (all optional), choose a plan, and resolve your first issue. It disappears once the required steps are done.
  5. Fix your next best moveOpen the top priority, follow the steps and run another scan to confirm it's solved. One fix at a time, your store gets closer to fully optimised.

Automatic scans start after your first “Run scan”. Installing the app or starting a trial never triggers a scan on its own.

03

The health score

One number from 0 to 100. It's 100 minus the points still open in your priorities, so every fix moves it up.

At risk0–49Let's turn the lights on.
Getting brighter50–79A few bulbs to change.
Glowing80–100Your store is glowing.

How points are counted

  • Weighted by priority. Every check has a maximum it can take from your score, set by how directly it costs you sales (see the table below).
  • The first items count most. Within that maximum, the first affected items remove the most points and each extra one a little less. One sold-out best-seller weighs more than 50 variants without a SKU, and your score doesn't jump just because a full scan counted more products than a quick one.
  • How the parts add up. First, ads take a share of the score: up to 20%, and only as much as your sales depend on them. No ad spend means no share at all. The rest of the score comes from your store and Google: 60% store and 40% Google with Merchant Center connected, or 100% store without it.
    Example: ads share 10%. The other 90% is split 60/40, so the score is 54% store, 36% Google and 10% ads.
  • Ignored problems don't count. You decide what's relevant.
PriorityChecksMost it can take
1 · Losing sales nowSold-out best-sellers, paid orders waiting to ship, best-sellers with sizes sold out, a store that's slow on phones, missing returns, shipping, privacy or contact pages, abandoned checkouts with no reminder email (up to 12)15–22 points each
2 · Conversion and trustProducts missing from your online store or without images, thin descriptions, weak photos, supplier text, products that don't say their size, no size guide, missing tracking, wrong sale prices, live products nobody can buy, conversion boosters, abandoned checkout emails that bring few shoppers back (up to 5)5–10 points each
3 · HousekeepingVariants without a SKU, apps slowing the store, no About us, FAQ or terms3–4 points each

Google Merchant Center keeps its own rule: each problem type removes points by impact, with a cap.

Conversion boosters count by booster, up to 8.5 points in total: product reviews 3, express checkout 2, recommended products 1.5, sticky Add to cart 1, email sign-up 1. A free shipping message is optional (not every store offers free shipping) and takes no points. “What's slowing your pages down” explains your speed score and takes no points of its own.

Apps count very little: an app switched off in your theme and two apps doing the same job take at most 1 point each. Code left behind by removed apps takes none, because its effect on speed already counts in your speed score.

While a scan runs

The quick scan only sees your first 100 products, so its score isn't final. While the full scan runs, the lamp blinks and the score shows “Counting your score…”; the number appears when the full scan ends.

Points gained and lost

“Since your last scan”, “Since your last visit” and the “+N points” celebration only count what changed in your store. A new check added by MerchLight, Google when it couldn't be read on both scans, and daily changes in your ads' ROAS don't move these numbers.

Sub-scores

Below the main score, four tiles show Store, Google Merchant, Google Ads and Meta Ads on their own. Tap any tile to jump to its details. A channel with no ad spend shows Lights off and doesn't count. Early ad scores show “Early read” and firm up as spend and sales data grow.

05

The Issues page

Every problem MerchLight found, with the full list of affected items.

  • Summary. Open issues, high-impact issues, checks passed and issues resolved since the last scan.
  • Search by product, order or SKU, and filter by type (Google Merchant, Stock, Product pages, Speed, Trust pages, Conversion boosters, Abandoned checkouts, Apps, Images, SKU, Orders, Google Ads, Meta Ads) and by impact. Each filter shows how many issues it holds. Links from the Overview open the right filter with the issue already open; “See all issues” opens them all.
  • Open any issue to see why it matters, how to resolve it, and each affected item with a direct button: “Restock in Shopify”, “Open order”, “Open in Google Ads” and so on.
  • Export CSV Pro on every issue: download its list of affected products or orders as a spreadsheet for Excel or Google Sheets, with a link to each one in your Shopify admin. Use it to work through a long list or to send it to someone who helps you. It's a working list: it doesn't import back into Shopify. No customer data is included.
  • Step by step. Every issue says where to click. Stock issues walk you through the Inventory box and the Draft status in Shopify; Google Merchant Center problems have their own guide per problem type; speed shows the exact image or app to blame.
  • What MerchLight checks lists every check by area: passed, to fix (tap it to open the issue), or not checked on this scan with the reason, for example “Only for stores that sell clothing or shoe sizes”.

When a problem isn't a problem for you

Already resolved, waiting to update

You fixed it, but the platform hasn't caught up yet (common with Google). It stops counting for 7 days, then comes back only if it's still there.

Not relevant for my store

Stops counting until you restore it. Ignored issues sit in their own list with a “Restore” button.

“I have stock”

Built for dropshipping. Your supplier has stock, but Shopify shows zero.

Inside the stock issues (sold-out products and best-sellers with sold-out sizes), tell MerchLight once and those products stop counting as out of stock for 30 days. Nothing changes in your store.

  • One product: the “I have stock” button on its row.
  • A whole supplier or product type: group by Supplier or Product type and press “I have stock for all”. A supplier rule also covers products you add later.
  • Everything: “I have stock for all” at the top.

Fixing it in Shopify, step by step: press the yellow button to open the product; in the Inventory box type the quantity you have and save; if your supplier ships it, tick “Continue selling when out of stock”; if it won't come back, change Status to Draft (top right). For a sold-out size or colour, open that variant in the Variants list. On your next scan the product leaves the list.

Your rules appear in their own list with their end date and a “Remove” button. If you answered “A dropshipping supplier” to the lamp's question, sold-out products with no recent sales also count as Low impact.

Permanent fix: in Shopify, turn on “Continue selling when out of stock” for products your supplier ships. They'll never show as out of stock again.

08

Google Merchant Center

See why products don't show on Google, and the one change that fixes them.

Connect from Connections, sign in with Google and choose your Merchant Center account. MerchLight then reads product statuses and groups problems into three checks: settings blocking products, disapproved products and limited visibility. Each one has Google's reason, the countries affected and how to fix it.

A guide for each problem. The most common problems (shipping currency, missing shipping, misrepresentation, product page unavailable, text on the main image, image quality, barcode/GTIN, price or stock mismatch, business registration number, local listings and policy blocks) get a plain name and numbered steps that say exactly where to click, in Merchant Center or in Shopify, with a button to open the right page. Google's own wording stays underneath.

  • Checked every 6 hours. Google can take from a few hours to 3 days to review a fix, so MerchLight asks Google at most every 6 hours. Scans in between reuse the last answer, and the Overview tells you when Google was last checked.
  • Full product list on Basic and Pro: every affected product, not just a summary.
  • Export to CSV on Pro.

Google Ads & Meta Ads

Your campaigns next to your store, measured against what you actually earn.

Connect Google Ads with your Google account, or Meta Ads with Facebook, and choose the ad account. The Ads page shows each platform in its own tab:

  • Totals for today, 7, 15 or 30 days, or your own dates: active campaigns, spend, conversions or purchases, cost per conversion and ROAS.
  • Your ads period. Choose 7, 15 or 30 days (the lamp asks once ads are connected, and you can switch on the Ads card). Your ads score, the Google Ads and Meta Ads tiles and the Ads card all use it. 7 days reacts fast to changes; 30 days is steadier. Ad alerts always look at the last 7 days.
  • Every campaign with its status, budget, spend and ROAS, filterable by Active, Paused and Removed, with a link straight to Google Ads or Ads Manager.
  • Break-even ROAS. Tell MerchLight your average margin (“Change margin”) and it shows the ROAS where ads start making money. Campaigns below it lose money after product costs.

Ad alerts (spending with no sales, switched on but not spending) appear in Issues and lower that channel's ad score.

Connections & privacy

What MerchLight reads, and what it never touches.

Read-only

MerchLight never edits your store, products, Merchant Center or ad accounts.

No customer details

We don't read your customers' names, emails, phone numbers or addresses. Abandoned checkouts are read only as counts, values and products.

You stay in control

Disconnect any channel from Connections at any time. Sign-in tokens are stored encrypted.

Your language

MerchLight follows your Shopify admin language. To choose another one, go to Connections → Language. Email alerts use the same language.

Speed & Trust

Reads only your public store pages, like a shopper would. The page addresses are sent to Google PageSpeed Insights to measure speed.

Your theme

MerchLight reads your published theme to see which apps are switched on or off and what code they left behind. It never changes your theme and never uninstalls apps.

Deleted when you leave

Uninstall MerchLight and your store's data is deleted, as described in our privacy policy.

Questions & fixes

I fixed a Google problem, but it's still showing

Google can take from a few hours to 3 days to review a fix, and MerchLight checks Google every 6 hours. Mark the issue “Already resolved, waiting to update” so it stops counting while Google catches up. It comes back after 7 days only if it's still there.

An unshipped order isn't listed

Orders count as late once they've been paid for more than 48 hours. Orders from today will appear if they're still unshipped after that.

A product at zero stock isn't flagged as out of stock

MerchLight only flags products customers can't buy. If the product doesn't track quantity, has “Continue selling when out of stock” turned on, or one variant still has stock, shoppers can still order it.

“Run scan” is greyed out

You've reached your plan's manual scans: the free scan on Free, 2 a week on Basic, or the short 5-minute pause on Pro. The message above the button says when you can scan again.

Ad figures don't match my store's sales

Spend, conversions and ROAS are reported by Google and Meta with their own attribution rules, so they can differ from the orders your store records.

My score changed after the October 2026 update, but I didn't change anything

The score now weighs most what costs you sales (like sold-out best-sellers), and less what's housekeeping (like SKUs). We also added the Speed & Trust checks. Your Overview says so on the first scan after the update, and these changes never trigger alert emails on their own.

Speed shows “Testing your speed…”

A speed test takes up to a minute per page, so it runs in the background after a scan and appears on the next one. It's repeated every 3 days. Stores with a password or that are closed can't be tested.

A trust page is flagged, but my store has it

MerchLight looks at Shopify's policy pages and at the links in your menu and footer. If your page has an unusual name or lives somewhere else, mark the issue “Not relevant for my store”, or add the link to your footer so shoppers find it too.

Meta Ads says “Reconnect”

Facebook sign-ins expire from time to time. Go to Connections and press Reconnect Meta Ads. Your settings are kept.

Need a hand?

Email support@merchlight.com, and include your store's .myshopify.com address so we can help faster.
